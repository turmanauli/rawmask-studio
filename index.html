Photo editor for iPhone

RawMask Studio

Edit RAW photos from your Photos library with precise masks and careful adjustments. Everything, including automatic object masking, runs on your iPhone.

Get support Privacy policy

What you can do

  • Edit RAW and regular photos

    Open a photo from Apple Photos and adjust framing, light, color, detail, focus, and effects with a live preview.

  • Automatic object masks

    An image segmentation model built into the app finds objects in your photo so you can edit them on their own.

  • Manual masks

    Add radial, linear, brush, and rectangle masks. Move and resize them, paint to add or subtract, and feather the edges.

  • Presets

    Save adjustments you like as presets and apply them to other photos.

  • Save and export

    Save your edit to Photos, save a copy, or export a JPEG, HEIF, or TIFF file through the share sheet.

  • Private by design

    No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app never sends your photos or data to us or to anyone else.

Requirements: iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Questions or problems? Visit the support page. Details about how the app handles your photos are in the privacy policy.
