Frequently asked questions

How do I open a photo?

Tap the photo button at the top left of the editor and choose Open photo. The Photos picker opens, and you choose one photo. RawMask Studio edits one photo at a time. Live Photos are not currently shown in the picker.

Which photos can I edit?

RAW photos that Apple's built-in RAW support can read, including Apple ProRAW and RAW files from many cameras, as well as regular photos such as HEIC and JPEG.

Why does the app ask for Photos access?

RawMask Studio opens photos from, and saves edits back to, your Apple Photos library, so iOS asks for your permission. You can allow full access or limited access to only the photos you pick. Either way, the app only opens the photo you choose. See the privacy policy for details.

I chose limited access and cannot find a photo

With limited access, the app can only open the photos you have allowed. To allow more photos:

  • In the app, tap More options (the ... button), then under Photos access tap Manage allowed photos, or
  • open Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > RawMask Studio and change the selection or choose Full Access.

The app says Photos access is off

Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > RawMask Studio, choose Limited Access or Full Access, then return to the app and open a photo again.

How do masks work?

Adjustments apply either to the whole photo or to a mask. Open the photo button menu at the top left: use Edit target to choose what you are editing, and Add mask to add a Radial, Linear, Brush, or Rectangle mask. You can move and resize a mask, paint to add to or subtract from it, and soften its edge with Feather.

What are automatic object masks?

RawMask Studio includes an image segmentation model that finds objects in your photo, such as people, animals, and everyday things, and offers each one as a mask you can edit on its own. The model runs entirely on your iPhone. If it does not pick up the object you want, use a manual mask instead, or adjust Detection confidence and Mask threshold under More options > Advanced > Object detection.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Editing and automatic masks work offline. The one exception is a photo stored in iCloud Photos whose original is not on your iPhone: Photos has to download the original from iCloud before it can be edited.

How do I save or export my edit?

Tap More options (the ... button) and go to Save and export:

  • Save edit to Photos updates the photo in your library. Photos keeps the original, so you can go back to it later with Revert in the Photos app.
  • Save a copy to Photos adds the edited image as a new photo and leaves the original unchanged.
  • Export photo creates a JPEG, HEIF, or TIFF file in the size and quality you choose and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files or send it to another app.

Where are my presets stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. You can delete presets in the app, and deleting the app removes them.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Does RawMask Studio collect my data?

No. There is no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and the app does not send your photos or data to us or to anyone else. Read the full privacy policy.
